How to mint

  1. Get a wallet. MetaMask or any EVM wallet works.
  2. Fund it with ETH on Robinhood Chain — this pays for your mint(s) and gas.
  3. Connect on robiscriptions.com and pick how many mints, up to 200 at once.
  4. Approve in your wallet. One transaction, no contract, no whitelist.

Why it matters

  1. No contract. The token exists purely as on-chain calldata — the inscription itself is the asset.
  2. Fully public mint. No team allocation, no presale, no insiders.
  3. First mover. The first RSC-20 protocol token to mint on Robinhood Chain.
  4. Verifiable supply. Every mint is a transaction anyone can audit on the explorer.

    The RSC-20 protocol
    
      
    How the token lives on-chain. Each operation is inscribed as a data URI in a transaction sent to the owner.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        i.
        
    deploy Create the token
    
        
    Sets the ticker, the max supply and the amount per mint. The first deploy of a ticker is the one that counts.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        ii.
        
    mint Mint units
    
        
    Sent to the Robiscriptions mint address with the ETH price. The wallet that signs is credited, as long as supply remains.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        iii.
        
    transfer Send a balance
    
        
    Sent to the transfer recipient and debited from the sender's balance.
